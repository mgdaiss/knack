Ava, Leo and the Brave Little Wave

The waves looked very big from the sand. Leo held Pip's leash tight and whispered, "What if the water is too deep?"

Ava knelt down beside him. "Big waves are just small waves that practiced," she said. "Let's practice with a small one first."

Just then, a tiny wave rolled up and tickled their toes, and Pip barked hello.
