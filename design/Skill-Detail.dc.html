Form Filler

Never write your address on a form again.
By Knack · Runs on your Mac · Works with any fillable PDF

How it works

1
Set up your household once
Names, birthdays, addresses, insurance, doctors, emergency contacts, and your signature.
2
Drop in a form
Drag a PDF onto the icon, or let it catch forms that arrive by email. It fills in everything it knows.
3
Answer the rest and sign
It highlights only what it couldn't answer. Sign, then save, print, or reply with it attached.

Good to know

The AI only sees a form's field labels. Your answers never leave this Mac.
Your original PDF is never changed. It saves a filled copy.
Scanned paper forms are coming in a later version.
Before you install
This skill will be able to:
Open PDFs you give it
Files you drop in, plus new PDFs in Downloads.
Spot forms in email
Optional. Gmail PDF attachments only, read-only.
Keep your household profile
Encrypted on this Mac, in your Keychain.
It will never:
Send your answers to the AI
Submit or email a form without your OK
Sign anything for you
